Methodological Highlights

  • Scale Invariance: Linear ridge regression trained on aggregated features generalizes across spatial resolution layers.
  • Mean Anchoring: Sub-regional delegation estimates strictly preserve exact official governorate totals under population weighting.
  • Grouped CV: 5-fold cross-validation grouped by governorate prevents spatial data leakage.
